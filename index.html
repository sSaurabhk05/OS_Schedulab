    Assumptions, limitations, and contribution

    Single CPU, known burst times, zero timed context-switch cost, user-declared process class, and offline simulation. The best-fit policy is an oracle for this known workload; it is not a claim about deployment in a live kernel.

    Contribution: Explainable Adaptive Meta-Scheduler—an educational integration of workload profiling, objective-aware policy comparison, readable rule traces, and regret analysis. It is not a new base scheduling algorithm.

    SC1–SC6 are checked against deterministic, hand-calculated workloads.
